Care guide
Your spider hatches from an egg sac (hold Pet on it to warm it) and grows over real days: each molt needs time and meals.
1 Observe – rest the cursor near your spider to be greeted by its pedipalps; flick it fast and it bolts for cover.
2 Prey – click to drop a fly. 3 Mist – click to spray water droplets.
4 Pet – press and hold on its body.
N night · S senses overlay · C CPG network · B brain · M sound. Audio unlocks on first click.
