Care guide
Every egg is a mystery: it could hatch a wolf spider, a huntsman, an orb weaver, a jumping spider, a black widow, or (rarely) a tarantula or a scorpion. Hold Pet on the egg to warm it. Your critter grows over real days: each molt needs time and meals.
1 Observe – rest the cursor near your critter and it reaches out to greet you; flick it fast and it bolts for cover.
2 Prey – click to drop a fly. 3 Mist – click to spray water droplets.
4 Pet – press and hold on its body.
